How The App Works

A rule becomes active when its watched status change or optional entry conditions match on the ticket. After the approval request is sent, the ticket can be moved to Closed automatically while responses continue to be tracked in the sidebar.

  • Create approval rules with watched statuses and any number of entry conditions built from ticket fields.
  • Send approval requests to agents or external email recipients from any Freshdesk sender mailbox.
  • External approval pages are hosted by Drive Automation, so customers do not need to configure any approval bridge settings.
  • Track approval progress in the ticket sidebar and capture the automatic ticket-close outcome alongside each approval request.
